# AlumioDTU 🎓

AlumioDTU is a full-stack alumni networking platform built to connect DTU students with alumni for mentorship, referrals, internships, networking, and career guidance.

Most students enter college believing that opportunities will automatically come through placements or academics alone. Reality hits later. Students struggle to find proper guidance, industry connections, internships, referrals, and mentors who can genuinely help them navigate careers.

At the same time, alumni are often willing to help juniors but there is no centralized and structured platform that connects both sides efficiently.

Students usually face problems like:

- Not knowing whom to approach for guidance
- Cold messaging random people on LinkedIn and getting ignored
- Missing internship opportunities due to lack of connections
- No proper mentorship roadmap
- Confusion regarding career paths, tech stacks, higher studies, and placements
- Lack of communication between seniors, alumni, and juniors
- Opportunities getting limited to only highly connected students
- Information being scattered across WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, LinkedIn posts, and spreadsheets

AlumioDTU aims to solve these problems by creating a dedicated ecosystem where DTU students and alumni can interact, collaborate, and grow together in one organized platform.

The goal is simple:
Build a strong student-alumni network that actually helps people instead of existing as a dead directory nobody uses.

---

## 🚀 Features

Currently, the platform provides:

- Student and Alumni Authentication
- Alumni Search & Filtering
- Mentorship and Referral Requests
- Internship & Job Opportunity Sharing
- Responsive UI Design
- User Profile Management
- Community Networking Features
